What

What is TimeCapsule

TimeCapsule is a digital place built around a different relationship with technology - one that uses its possibilities without letting its logic determine how we think, communicate and relate to the world.

Rather than prioritising speed, efficiency and endless content, it makes room for reflection, dialogue and sustained human participation. People can start discussions, share experiences and ideas, raise questions and challenge perspectives.

Through repeated participation, trust develops, relationships take shape and ideas evolve.

At its heart is the understanding that knowledge is more than information: it emerges through human experience, reflection and dialogue.

Encountering different perspectives deepens our understanding of ourselves, our societies and the world we inhabit.

TimeCapsule preserves this experience as a lasting record of ideas, knowledge and discussions, together with the contexts they emerge from. Each contribution carries its history and authorship, and the possibility of meaningful exchange. Together, they form an evolving human imprint: an inheritance that future generations can encounter, question and build upon.

Technology serves human expression rather than replacing it. TimeCapsule is a shared human space where people meet, think, communicate and develop understanding together, preserving what each generation has to share for those who follow.

How

How to use TimeCapsule

TimeCapsule gives people the space and tools to develop ideas, connect around common interests and build communities through active participation. Shared interests and exchanged perspectives create, over time, a sense of belonging.

Its environment combines human interaction with AI capabilities. AI tools help people follow discussions, discover relevant contributions, overcome language barriers, organise shared knowledge and prepare activities.

This makes participation more accessible and collaboration easier, while judgement, creativity and initiative remain with people.

Within TimeCapsule, people can:

Host and participate
Organise and take part in live discussions, talks, lectures and workshops to exchange knowledge, explore ideas and learn from one another.
Turn ideas into shared projects
Connect with others, discover common ground, develop initiatives and put collective ideas into practice.
Preserve and pass on knowledge
Build a lasting record of ideas, experiences and discussions, with authorship and original context intact, so that future generations can revisit it and build upon it.

Why

Why TimeCapsule

Technology has changed not only how we communicate, but the conditions under which we think, learn, work and relate to one another. Artificial intelligence is accelerating this change, increasingly taking part in activities once shaped by human judgement, creativity and experience. The question is no longer what technology can do, but what place we want it to have in our lives, and what we risk losing when its capabilities begin to define our purposes.

TimeCapsule is a place where humans meet through technology, not a place where technology simulates the presence of humans. What is disappearing is not connectivity, but the human presence that gives connectivity its meaning.

We can exchange more content without gaining understanding, communicate more often without building deeper relationships, and access vast information without acquiring knowledge. More communication does not automatically mean more meaningful participation.

AI can extend human capabilities by generating answers, processing information and performing cognitive tasks. But producing an answer is not the same as understanding why a question matters.

Within TimeCapsule, AI assists people and never impersonates them: members are real people, not bots, and human participation is never replaced by artificial activity. Human judgement, responsibility and experience remain central.

Purpose

Why it exists

TimeCapsule emerges from the need for a digital environment that is safe, respectful and built around genuine participation, where trust, privacy and mutual respect allow people to express themselves and engage with different perspectives. Safety is essential, because meaningful communication cannot develop without a sense of security. Within it, sustained conversations, shared learning, communities and collective creation can grow.

Ideas also deserve continuity. They should not disappear into the constant flow of digital content; their authorship, history and context should stay accessible for others to revisit, question and develop.

The purpose is not simply to connect more people or produce more content. It is to create a digital environment where technology serves human connection, shared knowledge and collective creation, while preserving the freedom, dignity and safety of the people who give it meaning.

Manifesto

We believe

  1. We believe technology should serve human life, not define its purposes. It can remain a means, without becoming the horizon within which everything acquires its meaning.

  2. We believe technological progress should expand human possibilities without reducing human existence to productivity, efficiency, calculation or measurable performance.

  3. We believe intelligence is more than the capacity to process information or produce answers. Human thought requires experience, imagination, interpretation, independent judgement and the freedom to question.

  4. We believe knowledge develops through observation, dialogue, disagreement, reflection and the encounter of different perspectives. Its value lies not only in what we know, but in how we come to understand.

  5. We believe communication is more than the transmission of content. It requires attention, reciprocity, trust and a willingness to encounter others.

  6. We believe participation must be active and meaningful. People should remain contributors to the knowledge, ideas and decisions that shape their shared world, rather than passive consumers of automated outputs.

  7. We believe communities grow through the continued involvement of their members. Through shared experiences, conversations and collective initiatives, people develop relationships, mutual understanding and the capacity to create together.

  8. We believe artificial intelligence should help people communicate, explore ideas, overcome barriers, organise knowledge and collaborate, extending human capabilities without replacing human judgement, initiative, authorship or responsibility.

  9. We believe human knowledge and experience deserve to be preserved, with their authorship, history and context intact, so that future generations can question and build upon them.

We stand for a digital environment in which technology brings human possibilities closer to fulfilment: where people participate rather than merely consume, understand rather than simply process, create together rather than produce in isolation, and build relationships that give meaning to the world they share.
