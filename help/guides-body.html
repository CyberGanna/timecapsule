Guide 1 of 5

Your first table

A table is a small live conversation about one question. Here is how to sit at your first one.

  1. Find a table

    Open Tables to see what is happening now, what starts soon and what comes later. Tap a card to read the question and see who is hosting and who is coming.

    Is free will an illusion?Gregory · Starts in 12 min

  2. Save a chair

    If the table starts later, choose Save a chair. You can sit as your first name or an alias, and we remind you before it starts. If plans change, give up your chair in My tables so someone else can sit.

    Save a chair

  3. Join when it starts

    When the table is live, choose Join table. A quick camera and microphone check comes first, then you sit down with the others.

    Join table

  4. Talk and listen

    It is an open discussion: no turns to wait for. On the side you can follow the live transcript, write private notes and send a private message to anyone at the table.

    TranscriptMessagesPeopleNotes

  5. Rate and award

    When the table ends, say how the discussion was and award your 8 TimeCoins to the people who inspired you. If you leave in a hurry, the bell reminds you the next day.

    Mind-blowingPleasantNot so inspiring

Nobody needs your full name. Listening counts too: you can stay quiet for a while and speak when you are ready.

Guide 2 of 5

Hosting a table

Hosting means bringing a question and keeping the conversation kind and moving. You don't need to be an expert, only curious.

  1. Pick a question

    Choose Create a table, pick a topic and write what you want to discuss. Open, short questions work best, because nobody can answer them alone.

    + Create a table

  2. Choose when and who

    Start now, later today or on a date you pick. Open tables appear on the Agenda for anyone to join. Private tables stay off the Agenda, and only people with your invite link can join.

    OpenPrivate

  3. Set the table

    Choose up to eight chairs and the language you will speak. Decide whether to keep a transcript of the discussion. A transcript goes to the public library only if everyone at the table agrees.

    Seats 8Keep a transcript of the discussion

  4. Invite people

    Share the table link, or use Invite friends to save a chair for someone next to yours: when you sit together you both gain 5 TimeCoins. Before the table, My tables shows who is coming and lets you message your guests.

  5. Lead the conversation

    Open with the question and a short welcome, and bring in the quieter voices. Your one control is the notes: questions guests send with Ask come to you, and you decide when to put them to the table. Private messages between guests are theirs, and you can't see or stop them.

    Ask the tableLater

  6. Close well

    A few minutes before the end, ask each chair for one last thought. Then choose End table. Your guests rate the table and can award you TimeCoins.

    End table

Hosting good tables again and again is the surest way to become Trusted.

Guide 3 of 5

TimeCoins

TimeCoins are how people say thank you after a table. You earn them only from other people, never with money.

  1. 8 coins after every table

    After each table you finish, you get 8 TimeCoins to award. Split them between the host and the guests who inspired you. Coins you don't award expire after a few days.

    Gregory−4+
    Lena−2+
    2 left to award

  2. Coins you receive

    Every coin someone awards you adds to your Received total on your profile. It only goes up. It shows that people valued your voice, and it counts toward becoming Trusted.

  3. Coins you can use

    The coins you receive also go into a balance you can spend on extras, such as two more chairs at a table you host or a recap of a past table. Spending never lowers your Received total. Sitting, hosting and your own transcript stay free.

    See your TimeCoins

  4. Inviting friends

    Invite up to 10 friends a week to join TimeCapsule. They'll gain 2 TimeCoins, and you, too! Invite up to 10 friends a week to sit at your tables. They'll gain 5 TimeCoins, and you, too!

  5. What coins never do

    Reputation can't be bought: coins you might buy for extras in the future never count toward it. Coins can't be passed on outside an award, and there are no leaderboards.

Guide 4 of 5

Notes

During a table you can write notes on the side. They are private: only you can see them.

  1. Write a note

    Open the Notes tab and start typing. To catch a moment, tap the note icon next to a line in the transcript, and your note quotes it.

    Gregory: Maybe choosing is just noticing what we already are.Ask about habits. Are they choices we stopped seeing?
    KeepAskTranscript

  2. Three ways to use a note

    Keep

    Save the note for later. Kept notes wait for you in My tables, where you can export them as a text file.

    Ask

    Send the note as a question to the host, who decides when to put it to the table.

    Transcript

    Add the note to the transcript, under the line it quotes. You can take it out until the table ends.

  3. Nothing leaves without you

    A note stays private until you choose Ask or Transcript, and you confirm before a question is sent.

Guide 5 of 5

Report abuse

Most tables are kind. When one isn't, here is what you can do, and what we do.

  1. Leave if you need to

    If a table stops feeling safe, choose Leave table. You never have to explain, and you can report afterwards.

  2. Report the person

    Open their profile, tap the three dots next to the Follow button and choose Report. Pick a reason and, if you want, add a few words. Only our team reads it.

    Making me feel unsafe or followedHarassment or hateSpam or scamSomething else

  3. Block them too

    Also block is on by default when you report. A blocked person can't see your profile or tables, sit at yours, chat or follow you. They aren't told. You can unblock in Settings.

  4. What happens next

    Reports are private and the person is never told who sent one. Our team reviews every report. Someone who keeps crossing the line can lose their Trusted badge or be removed from TimeCapsule.

Hosts have no control over private messages, so report an unkind message yourself. The host can't do it for you.
