Getting started

What is TimeCapsule?

Small live tables where real people talk about one question at a time. Each table has up to eight chairs, a host and an hour or so of real conversation.

Does it cost anything?

No. Sitting at tables and hosting them are free.

Do I have to use my real name?

No. Use your first name or an alias. You can choose how you appear each time you save a chair.

Which languages can I use?

Each table shows its language before you sit down. The app itself is in English, Italian and Spanish: switch it in the menu under your photo or in Settings.

Where do I start?

Read Your first table in the Guides, then open Tables and save a chair at something that makes you curious.

At the table

How many people sit at a table?

Up to eight, including the host. Small tables give everyone time to speak.

What kinds of table are there?

Two: Open and Private. An Open table is on the Tables page and anyone can take a free chair. A Private table stays off the Tables page, and only people with the link can join. Either way, the person who creates the table hosts it.

What if I can't make it?

Give up your chair in My tables so someone else can sit. At a live table you can leave at any time with Leave table.

Is there a transcript?

Only when the host turns it on, and the table page tells you before you sit down. A transcript becomes public only if everyone at the table agrees.

Can I message someone privately during a table?

Yes. Open Messages and pick a person. Only the two of you see it. The host can't see or control private messages.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. A note leaves your Notes tab only when you choose Ask, which sends it to the host, or Transcript.

Hosting

How do I host a table?

Choose Create a table at the top of the page, write your question, pick a time and open it. The guide Hosting a table walks you through it.

Can I host a private table?

Yes. Choose Private under Who can join. The table stays off the Tables page and only people with your link can join.

What can the host control?

Only the notes. Questions guests send with Ask come to the host, who decides when to put them to the table. The host can't read or stop private messages.

How do I change or cancel my table?

Open My tables, find the table and use its menu to edit it, message your guests or cancel it. Your guests are told if you cancel.

What if someone is unkind at my table?

Bring the table back to the question. If it goes on, report the person from their profile. Reports are read by our team.

TimeCoins and Trusted

Can I buy TimeCoins?

Not yet. Today TimeCoins come only from people at your tables and from inviting friends. In the future you may be able to buy coins to pay for extra services, but bought coins never count toward your reputation.

What do I get for inviting friends?

Invite up to 10 friends a week to join TimeCapsule. They'll gain 2 TimeCoins, and you, too! Invite up to 10 friends a week to sit at your tables. They'll gain 5 TimeCoins, and you, too!

Why did some of my coins disappear?

The 8 coins you get to award after a table expire after a few days if you don't award them. Coins you have received never expire.

What does Trusted mean?

The Trusted badge shows that someone has taken part well over time: finished many tables, hosted with good ratings and received TimeCoins from many different people. Logins, messages and followers don't count.

How do I see my progress toward Trusted?

On your profile, tap the badge next to your name. Only you can see your progress.

Privacy and safety

Who can see which tables I join?

Visitors to your profile see only the open tables you host. Tables you join as a guest, private tables and when you were last online are never shown.

Is my data sold?

Never. Your data isn't sold, and it isn't mined for advertising.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile, tap the three dots next to the Follow button and choose Block or Report. Blocking is silent: they aren't told. You can see and undo blocks in Settings.

Who can chat with me?

You decide in Settings: everyone, only people you have sat with, or nobody.

Your account

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page choose Forgot password. We email you a link to set a new one.

How do I change my notifications?

In Settings, under Notifications. Reminders for tables you are about to join always ring the bell.

How do I download or delete my data?

In Settings, under Your data. You can download a copy of everything before you delete your account.
