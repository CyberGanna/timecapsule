## TimeCapsule | Participation, Autonomy and the Preservation of Understanding

Participation should never imply surrendering ownership of one's contribution. The preservation of collective understanding must coexist with the individual's freedom to participate, determine how their contribution is represented, and reconsider their decision. The conversation may become part of a shared intellectual history; participation in that history must remain a matter of individual choice.

The preservation of human understanding must begin with respect for the autonomy of the people who contribute to its development. Participation in a conversation should never imply surrendering control over one's contribution or how it becomes part of a shared intellectual history.

The decision to record a discussion must be established in advance through the table settings, ensuring that everyone knows the conditions before participating. Recording, however, does not automatically imply consent to retain or share the material. These are distinct decisions, each requiring clarity and agreement.

Every participant should retain control over how their contribution is represented. They may choose to participate under their name, anonymously or without having their contribution recorded. This choice should not be irreversible. Participants should be able to reconsider their decision before, during or after a discussion, including changing their attribution preference or withdrawing their contribution from the preserved material.

Similarly, the preservation of a discussion within a community should not automatically make it available to a wider audience. The decision to share material beyond its original context requires a separate agreement.

These principles recognise that human participation is not a resource to be extracted or an asset that the platform acquires. It is a voluntary contribution to a collective process of understanding.

TimeCapsule seeks to preserve the continuity of human understanding without compromising the autonomy of the individuals who make it possible. The value of what is preserved must never take precedence over the freedom of those who contribute.

## TimeCapsule | The Continuity of Human Understanding

Human understanding is not produced in isolation, nor is it confined to the moment in which it emerges. It develops through encounters, dialogue, disagreement, interpretation and the accumulated experience of others.

We inherit ideas, questions and ways of understanding the world, but we also transform them through our own encounters and contributions. Understanding is therefore not simply something we acquire; it is something that develops between people and across generations.

When conversations disappear, we lose not only their content but also part of the history through which understanding was developed - the questions that initiated an inquiry, the different perspectives that challenged it, the interpretations that emerged and the experiences that gave it meaning. Preserving conversations creates continuity between people who may never meet, allowing questions to remain open, knowledge to evolve and future generations to participate in an ongoing human inquiry.

Understanding is not a fixed outcome, but an ongoing process of development. It takes shape through dialogue, reasoning, disagreement, the exchange of experiences and perspectives, and the historical context in which these unfold. An archive preserves what happened. A knowledge repository preserves what was learned. TimeCapsule seeks to preserve the human process through which understanding develops - not merely the conclusions people reach, but also the dialogue, reasoning, disagreements and shared experiences through which ideas evolve and understanding takes shape.

Such preservation must never mean freezing knowledge, treating previous discussions as definitive or turning past interpretations into unquestionable truths. Its value lies precisely in keeping human thought open - open to questioning, reinterpretation, challenge and further contribution. What one conversation leaves unresolved can become the starting point of another. One good answer, can help us form an even better question. What one generation explores can be reconsidered and developed by the next.

TimeCapsule seeks to connect the intellectual and human contributions of different people and generations, allowing the understanding developed through dialogue to become part of an evolving body of shared knowledge, open to future questioning, interpretation and contribution.
