    How Trusted works

    Trusted is earned at tables. It grows when you finish tables, host good discussions, and when many different people award you TimeCoins.

    • Coins from many people count more than many coins from a few.
    • Older tables slowly count less, so trust reflects how you take part now.
    • Logins, messages, follows and followers never count.
    • Others only see the Trusted chip. Your progress stays here, and there is no ranking.
