5 · Show under my name

From the table's ⋯ menu in My tables, once it has ended. One-way: the people at that table will see the change.

Show "Grief and the holidays" under your name?

Quiet Heron becomes Gabriele at this table. You can't switch back.

  • The transcript, Messages and guest list show your name.
  • 3 feedbacks, 4.7 ★ and 6 TimeCoins move to your profile.
  • Visitors to your profile still won't see this table.
  • Table-mates aren't notified, but can see the change.
