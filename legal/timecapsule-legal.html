Community guidelines

TimeCapsule is small tables of real people talking. These few rules keep every table kind, honest and safe, for guests and hosts alike.

Draft of 8 November 2026

Draft for review. A lawyer must check these texts before launch, and the details in square brackets still need filling in.

In short

Real people, kind talk, no selling, and what is said at a table stays at the table.

Be a real person

Every chair is taken by a human being. One account per person, and it is yours alone.

  • A first name or an alias is fine. Pretending to be a real someone else is not.
  • No bots, no fake accounts, and no AI speaking or writing for you at a table.
  • If you share expertise, be honest about what you know and what you don't.

Listen as much as you talk

  • Leave room for others, especially for people who are quieter or new.
  • Disagree with ideas, never attack the person. Kind disagreement is welcome here.
  • Stay with the question of the table. Side topics belong in a private message or a new table.

Keep it safe

These are never allowed, on camera, in the transcript, in notes sent with Ask or in private messages:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or following someone from table to table against their wishes.
  • Hate or contempt aimed at people for who they are: origin, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or age.
  • Sexual content, nudity, or anything that sexualises minors.
  • Encouraging violence, self-harm or illegal acts.

Respect privacy

  • Don't record, film or screenshot a table. The only record is the transcript, and only when the host keeps one.
  • A transcript becomes public only if everyone at the table agrees. Until then, what was said stays with the people who were there.
  • Never share someone's surname, address, workplace or photos without their permission.

Private messages

Anyone at a table can send a private message to anyone else. The same rules apply there. The host can't see or control private messages, so if one is unkind, report it yourself.

If you host a table

  • Welcome everyone who takes a chair, and start and end on time.
  • You are a host, not a judge. Keep the conversation kind and moving, and bring questions sent with Ask to the table when it fits.
  • Don't use a table to sell, recruit or fundraise.
  • If someone breaks these rules, you can remove them from your table. Then report what happened so our team can look at it.

TimeCoins are thank-yous

  • Give coins to people who inspired you, never in exchange for anything.
  • Don't ask for coins, trade them, or agree with others to tip each other.
  • Coins you receive are your reputation, and reputation can never be bought. Gaming coins can cost you your Trusted badge.

No spam, no scams

No advertising, chain messages, links to schemes, or asking people for money or personal details.

When something goes wrong

  • You can always leave a table. You never have to explain.
  • To report someone, open their profile, tap the three dots next to the Follow button and choose Report. Reports are private.
  • You can block anyone. They can't see your profile or tables, sit at yours, chat or follow you, and they aren't told.
  • If someone is in danger right now, contact local emergency services first.

What we do

A person on our team reads every report. Depending on what happened, we may send a reminder of these rules, remove content, pause the Trusted badge for 30 days, suspend an account or close it for good. If we act on your account, we tell you why, and you can ask us to look again within 30 days.

Terms of service

The agreement between you and TimeCapsule, in plain words. By creating an account you accept these terms and the community guidelines.

Draft of 8 November 2026

Draft for review. A lawyer must check these texts before launch, and the details in square brackets still need filling in.

In short

You must be at least 16. You own what you say. Reputation can never be bought. You can leave whenever you like.

1. Who we are

TimeCapsule is run by [Company name], [address], [country], from the team behind TimeRepublik. In these terms, "we" means that company and "you" means the person using TimeCapsule.

2. Who can join

  • You must be at least 16 years old.
  • One account per person. Accounts are for people, not companies or bots.
  • Keep your sign-in safe and your email up to date. You are responsible for what happens on your account.

3. Tables

  • A table is a live conversation hosted by the member who created it. Open tables appear on the Agenda; private tables can only be joined with the link.
  • Hosts are members like you, not our staff. We don't check what is said in advance and we can't promise that every table takes place.
  • Video and sound are streamed live and are not recorded by us.

4. Your words and transcripts

  • What you say and write stays yours.
  • When a host chooses Keep a transcript of the discussion, you allow us to store it and show it to the people who were at the table, with each line credited to the name or alias you used.
  • A transcript goes into the public library only if everyone at the table agrees. Then you allow us to keep and show it there, with its authorship and date, as part of a lasting record for future readers.
  • You can ask us at any time to replace your name with "A guest" in a public transcript.
  • Your notes are private. Only you see them, unless you send one with Ask or add it to the transcript.

5. TimeCoins

  • TimeCoins are thank-yous between members. The coins you receive show on your profile as your reputation.
  • Reputation can never be bought, sold or transferred. Coins received as thanks can't be traded or moved outside TimeCapsule.
  • In the future you may be able to buy TimeCoins to pay for extra services. Coins you buy never count toward your reputation.
  • Coins given to you to award after a table expire if you don't award them within a few days.
  • We may change how coins work. We will tell you before a change that affects coins you already have.

6. Trusted

Trusted and Trusted host are earned through repeated, kind participation at tables. They are a sign of good standing, not a guarantee about a person. We may pause or remove them if the community guidelines are broken.

7. AI and automatic tools

  • We use automatic tools to write the live transcript and captions, tidy notes and translate. They can make mistakes.
  • AI on TimeCapsule only assists. It never takes a chair, never speaks as a member and never pretends to be a person.

8. How you behave

Follow the community guidelines. Don't break the law, harm other members, or try to disrupt, copy or reverse-engineer the service.

9. When we step in

If you break these terms, we may remove content, limit or suspend your account, or close it. We tell you why, except where the law or someone's safety prevents it, and you can ask us to look again.

10. Leaving

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Your profile, notes and chats go with it. Lines you spoke in a public transcript stay in the library, credited to "A guest".

11. The service

TimeCapsule is new and still growing, and it is free during the pilot. We work hard to keep it running well but can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for indirect losses or for what members say at tables. Nothing here limits your rights as a consumer.

12. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms in a way that matters, we tell you in the app and by email at least 30 days before. If you don't agree, you can delete your account.

13. Law and disputes

These terms are governed by the law of [country]. Disputes go to the courts of [city], unless the law where you live gives you the right to go to your local courts.

14. Contact

Questions about these terms: [hello@timecapsule.example]

Privacy policy

What we collect, why, who can see it, and how you stay in control. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Draft of 8 November 2026

Draft for review. A lawyer must check these texts before launch, and the details in square brackets still need filling in.

In short

No ads, no data sales, no recordings of calls. You can download or delete your data at any time.

1. Who is responsible

[Company name], [address], [country] is responsible for your data. Write to [privacy@timecapsule.example] about anything on this page.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your email, the name or alias you choose, your photo if you add one, and how you sign in (Apple, Google or email).
  • Profile: your city, bio, the topics you talk about, and the books and films you add.
  • Activity: tables you save, join or host, ratings, feedback and TimeCoins given and received.
  • Payments, if you buy TimeCoins in the future: what you bought and when. Card details stay with the payment provider.
  • Conversations: transcripts, only when the host keeps one; your notes; your chat messages.
  • Safety: reports you send or that are made about you, and blocks.
  • Technical: device and browser type, IP address and error logs, to keep the service working and secure.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't record the video or sound of tables.
  • We don't use your conversations to train AI models.
  • We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. We only use what is needed to keep you signed in and remember your choices, such as your language.

4. Why we use it

  • To run TimeCapsule for you: your account, tables, reminders and transcripts.
  • To keep tables safe: reviewing reports and preventing fake accounts and abuse.
  • To suggest tables and people, using your topics, books and films. Only TimeCapsule uses them.
  • With your consent: making a transcript public, and optional emails. You can change your mind at any time.
  • When the law requires it.

5. Who can see what

  • Other members see your name or alias, photo, city, topics, ratings, and the open tables you host.
  • Nobody else sees the tables you only joined, your private tables, when you were last seen, your notes or your messages to others.
  • People at a table see and hear you live, and can read its transcript if one is kept.
  • Our team only looks at conversations when a report asks us to, or when the law requires it.
  • Service providers that help us run TimeCapsule (hosting, live video, transcription, email) process data only on our instructions: [list of providers].

6. Where your data lives

Your data is stored in [Switzerland or the EU]. If a provider handles it elsewhere, we use the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Private transcripts: until the host deletes them, or you delete your account.
  • Public transcripts: kept in the library as a lasting record. You can ask for your name to be removed.
  • Table group chats close after 7 days. Private chats stay until you delete them or your account.
  • Reports: [1 year] after they are closed. Technical logs: [30 days].

8. Your rights

  • See, download and correct your data. Download your data is in Settings.
  • Delete your account and your data. Delete account is in Settings.
  • Object to how we use your data, or withdraw a consent you gave.
  • Complain to a data protection authority, such as the FDPIC in Switzerland or the authority in your EU country.

9. Young people

TimeCapsule is for people aged 16 and over. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we close it and delete their data.

10. Keeping it safe

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, limit who on our team can access it, and lock sign-in after repeated failed attempts. If a breach puts you at risk, we tell you quickly.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we tell you in the app and by email before it applies.
