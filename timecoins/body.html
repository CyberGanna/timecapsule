          History

            How TimeCoins work

            A proposal for the coin economy. Prices and numbers are placeholders to try in the pilot.

            Two numbers, not one

            If coins are reputation and also money, every purchase would make you look less trusted, and people would hold back from using them. So each person has two numbers that grow together but move apart once you spend.

            To award8 after every table you finish. You can only give them away. Unawarded coins expire after 3 days.
            →
            ReceivedEvery coin someone awards you. It only goes up. It is the number on your profile and part of the Trusted score.

            To useThe same coins, as a balance you can spend on extras. Using them lowers this number only.
            Like air miles: the miles that give you status are counted apart from the miles you spend on a flight. Spending never lowers what you received.

            Where coins come from

            • Awards after a table. The only source of reputation. Counted in Received and in To use.
            • Inviting friends. 2 when a friend signs in, 5 when you sit at a table together. These go to To use only, marked Welcome, and never count as reputation.
            • Backed table ideas. When you host a topic people pledged coins to, you receive the pledges. To use only.
            • No daily gifts and no login streaks. In the future you may be able to buy coins for extras: they go to To use only and never count as reputation.

            What coins can buy

            A guest receives about 4 coins per table and a host about 30, so guest extras cost about one table and host extras cost less than one hosted table.

            What stays free, and why

            You asked about paying for Notes or the transcript. My advice is to keep the basics free and charge for what sits on top. The transcript is the lasting record of the discussion, and it only works if everyone keeps one. If notes cost coins, newcomers, who have none, would take part less.

            • Free: sitting at any table, hosting with up to 8 chairs, the live transcript and your copy of it, private notes with Keep and Ask, rating and awarding, reading the public library, block and report, exporting or deleting your data.
            • Paid with coins: what costs us something to run, such as more chairs, longer video, live translation, AI recaps and polished library editions.

            What coins never do

            • They never turn bought coins into reputation. Received counts only awards from people at your tables, so reputation can never be bought.
            • They can't be sent to a person outside awards, or used to award more than 8 per table.
            • They can't buy a place in line, a better spot on the Tables page, or the Trusted badge.
            • No leaderboards and no ranking by coins.

            Keeping it balanced

            • Prices are set by us, and there is no market, so hoarding can't push prices up. That is why To use coins can safely never expire.
            • Each table finished creates 8 coins per person, and extras take coins back out. If balances pile up during the pilot, add extras rather than raise prices.
            • Caps keep tables small: at most 12 chairs and one extra half hour per table.
            • Coins people pledge to a topic go back to them if nobody hosts it within 30 days.

            Open questions

            • Prices. Are 10 coins for two more chairs and 20 for live captions right for the pilot?
            • TimeRepublik credits. Can old TimeRepublik credits turn into To use coins, but never into Received?
            • School tables. Which partner would receive coins people give to cover school tables?
